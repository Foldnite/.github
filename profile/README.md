# Foldnite

**Interactive STEM worlds. Collaborative missions. Science-inspired play.**

Foldnite explores ways to make science and engineering tangible through 3D environments, virtual equipment, teamwork, simulation, and mission-based problem solving.

**STEM** means Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. In Foldnite, these fields can appear as biology and molecular exploration, chemistry and materials, robotics, energy, climate, environmental systems, and quantitative challenges.

## How we describe progress

Design documents, planning estimates, prototypes, deployed services, and validated scientific results are different evidence classes. We aim to state clearly what exists, what is being tested, and what remains a proposal. An in-game score is not automatically a scientific validation result.

Foldnite is an independent project. References to third-party platforms, research tools, brands, or scientific methods do not imply ownership, partnership, or endorsement.
